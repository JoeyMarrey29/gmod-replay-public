# GMod Replay Public 0.1.53 (test build, 2026-11-04)

First public release of GMod Replay.

- No GitHub account or sign-in needed. Updates come from the public releases page.
- Recordings can be opened once they are at least 15 minutes old.
- Controls help page: press F1, or open Escape > Controls.
- Player cards: drag across any text to highlight and copy it, press Ctrl+C, or double-click a value to copy it.
- Click a vehicle to see who owns it, its registration and its current driver.
