# GMod Replay Public 0.2.0

Stable public release.

- No sign-in required. Updates check and download automatically on launch, then ask before installation.
- Recordings unlock 15 minutes after the recording ends.
- Press F1, or Escape > Controls, for all replay controls.
- Highlight and copy player details, or double-click a value to copy it.
- Select a vehicle to see its owner, registration and driver.
- Only this release's notes are bundled; historical notes are not included.
